# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Game server owners manage tenant-scoped resources in the Workspace Console. Platform Operators inspect global ownership and commercial state in the Platform Console, then operate regional execution through Region Operations. Users may work during routine setup or under incident pressure, on desktop or mobile, in light or dim environments.

## Product Purpose

GamePanel is a hosted control surface for purchasing, configuring, placing, and operating game servers across independently deployed Regions. Success means each user can understand their current scope, authority, and resource state without confusing global customer intent with regional or node-local execution.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. Copy is direct and operational. The interface is approachable to a first-time server owner while remaining legible and efficient for operators during failures.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic enterprise administration template, a neon gaming dashboard, a cyberpunk control room, or a decorative card gallery. Avoid gradients, glass panels, ornamental artwork, excessive shadows, unexplained infrastructure terminology, and copied legacy GamePanel visuals.

## Design Principles

1. Make operating scope explicit at every transition.
2. Separate customer intent from regional execution state.
3. Prefer readable hierarchy, tables, and whitespace over decorative containers.
4. Explain empty, partial, stale, unavailable, forbidden, and failed states in context.
5. Keep familiar controls consistent so attention stays on the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and one locale per rendered session. Theme supports light, dark, and system from the first shell.
